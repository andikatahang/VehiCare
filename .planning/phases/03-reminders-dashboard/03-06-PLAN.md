<objective>
**As a** vehicle owner, **I want to** open the app and see which vehicle needs attention first, **so that** I can act on the most urgent service without reading every card.

Turn the home vehicle list into the dashboard: every vehicle's card gains one status line carrying the last-service date and its most urgent reminder's due sentence, and the list itself is ordered by urgency instead of recency.

Purpose: this is the phase's at-a-glance surface and the only place the whole fleet is compared. The comparison must be the engine's, not a second sort written in a ViewModel — plans 02 and 03 already ship `ReminderStatus.evaluateAll` and `ReminderOrdering.sortVehicles`, and this plan consumes them unchanged. The single aggregated read is equally load-bearing: the naive implementation (one rule query per vehicle) is invisible at three vehicles and pathological at thirty, so the shape is fixed here as four flat flows combined in Kotlin.

**DSH-01 (amended 2026-10-23, user-approved).** Both facts the requirement names — *last service date* and *next due service* — render on the home screen, on the card's one approved status line (C-30 amended; CONTEXT D-59 amended): `Last service {date} · {due sentence}`, with ` · +N due` per D-59, falling back to `{type} · {sentence}` when the vehicle has no service entry, and `Last service {date} · No active reminders` (date segment omitted entirely when there is no entry) when every rule is off. The date segment reuses the existing `service_summary_last` string (`Last service %1$s`) and `ServiceDates.display` — no new resource — and the epoch already arrives with the `observeVehicleStats` read that feeds `evaluateAll`, so no query is added. Do not invent a second card line, a badge or a subtitle: the line stays one `Body` line, `maxLines = 1`, and the uniform card height (D-59) is untouched.

Output: `VehicleDashboardRow` (carrying `lastServiceEpochMs`) and the four-flow dashboard assembly in `VehicleListViewModel`, its `commonTest` suite, the one DSH-01 status line in `VehicleCard`, the pass-through in `VehicleListScreen`, and the updated `VehicleListViewModel` Koin factory.
</objective>
