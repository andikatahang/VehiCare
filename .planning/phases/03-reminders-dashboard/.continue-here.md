## Critical Anti-Patterns (do NOT repeat these)

| Pattern | Description | Severity | Prevention Mechanism |
|---------|-------------|----------|---------------------|
| Two databases in one process | A `CoroutineWorker` in `:shared` `androidMain` constructs its own `VehiCareDatabase`. SQLDelight's listener registry lives on the driver, so the worker's writes would succeed but never wake the UI's Flows — reminders would silently go stale with no error. Verified by source inspection, not yet reproduced at runtime. | advisory | `DatabaseProvider` is plan 03-01's deliverable; every non-Compose consumer must read the database through it. A plan that adds a second driver construction is wrong regardless of how convenient it looks. |
| Re-deriving the due state per surface | Formatting "when is this due" inside a composable, a worker, or a platform notification builder. Six surfaces render this sentence and would drift. | advisory | One pure `ReminderStatus` result (03-02) consumed by one `ReminderCopy` resolver (03-03). Both platforms resolve their notification body through the same resolver. |
| Past-dated `UNCalendarNotificationTrigger` | Scheduling a trigger whose date has passed fires it immediately — and again on every reschedule. | advisory | Schedule only the **next** due per rule; never walk a backlog of missed occurrences (also keeps under the 64-pending cap). |
| Weak iOS delegate retention | `UNUserNotificationCenter.delegate` is a weak reference; a delegate assigned without a strong owner deallocates and taps stop routing. | advisory | Retain it for the app's lifetime, assigned before KMP init. |
| Padding gaps in the UI contract | Adding a spacing value, colour, type size, icon or string not declared in `03-UI-SPEC.md`. | advisory | The spec's declared sets are exhaustive. `NotificationsOff`/`Alarm`/`Timer`/`Schedule`/`Speed` do not exist in `material-icons-core:1.7.3`; the off-state carries its meaning in words. |
