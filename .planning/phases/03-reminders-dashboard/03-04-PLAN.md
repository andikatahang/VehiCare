<objective>
Build the Reminders screen as a real vertical slice of its own: the list of a vehicle's reminder rules ordered by urgency, each row carrying its interval and its due state, with an in-place on/off switch and the Add entry point — plus the two pure pieces the editor depends on (single-select chips and the numeric form rules).

Purpose: this is the first surface where the phase's calculation becomes visible, and it is deliberately the simplest one — no form, no delete, no permission ask. Everything it shows comes from the pure engine and the one copy helper, so the same sentences will appear on the dashboard and in notifications without a second implementation. The single-select mode and the form rules land here because they are pure and the editor plan should not have to invent them while also building a form.

Output: `SelectionMode` + `selectServiceType` with the picker's new parameter and tap rule, `ReminderFormValidation`, `RemindersViewModel`/`RemindersUiState`, `ReminderCard`, `RemindersRoute`, the destination type and one Koin binding. The destination is registered in `App.kt` by plan 05 together with its entry point from Vehicle Detail — this plan deliberately does not add a graph node nothing can reach.
</objective>
