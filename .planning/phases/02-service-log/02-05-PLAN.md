## Phase Goal

**As a** vehicle owner, **I want to** log every service event — date, mileage, type, cost, notes, parts, workshop, and photos — and browse, edit, or delete any vehicle's full history, **so that** I have a trustworthy record of all maintenance performed.

> **Roadmap fidelity note:** the ROADMAP `**Goal:**` line for this phase is outcome-form, not user-story form. The story above is a faithful restatement of ROADMAP success criteria 1–5 and REQUIREMENTS SVC-01…SVC-07 — no scope was invented. If the user wants the canonical story committed to ROADMAP.md, run `/gsd-mvp-phase 2`.

<objective>
Build the Service history destination: the sortable, keyed list of a vehicle's entries, its empty/loading/error states, and the "Add service" entry point — plus the shared empty-state component both screens now use.

Purpose: this is the screen that makes the phase's data useful — the first place a user sees a vehicle's whole maintenance story at once, and the natural place from which to add the next entry. It is also where two carried-forward rules are easiest to break: reading `date · mileage` without duplicating a formatter, and binding rows by key when the list re-orders under the user.

Slice position (MVP): the browse half of the story (SVC-05) plus the history screen's own add path. Its rows open the detail built in 02-04 and its FAB/CTA open the form built in 02-03 — both destinations already exist, so the screen ships complete. The vehicle Detail's summary row, which is what *opens this screen*, lands in 02-06; nothing points at a missing destination in the meantime.

Output: the moved and parameterized `EmptyState`, the history ViewModel with its non-persisted sort state, the history screen with its FAB overlay and state matrix, the card row and the segmented sort control, and the registered `ServiceHistoryRoute`.
</objective>
