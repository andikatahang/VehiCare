## Phase Goal

**As a** vehicle owner, **I want to** add, view, edit, and delete my vehicles (with an optional photo) on both Android and iOS, **so that** I have a trusted offline record of every vehicle I own.

> Roadmap fidelity note: the ROADMAP `**Goal:**` line for Phase 1 is outcome-form, not user-story form. The story above is the MVP-mode rendering of it. Run `/gsd-mvp-phase 1` if the ROADMAP line should itself be rewritten into story form.

<objective>
Deliver VEH-02: the gallery-only photo capability, end to end. Author `PhotoStorage` as the single filesystem seam, add `PhotoField` to the existing Add/Edit form, drive the photo lifecycle from the ViewModel, and replace the no-op photo-deletion seam from plan 01-03 with the real `PhotoStorage::delete`.

Purpose: photos are the phase's only file-I/O surface and the only place where a design mistake becomes unrecoverable user-visible data loss — an absolute path renders broken after the next iOS install, and an un-deleted file grows storage forever. This plan concentrates on getting the *path contract* right (relative only, resolved fresh at read time) and on making every supersede/abandon path delete its file, because those are the two failures that no later phase can retroactively repair without a migration.

Output: a vehicle that can carry one optional photo, stored as a file in the app's private directory with only its relative path in SQLite, previewable on the form, replaceable, removable, and proven on a real filesystem.
</objective>
