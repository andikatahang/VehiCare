## Phase Goal

**As a** vehicle owner, **I want to** add, view, edit, and delete my vehicles (with an optional photo) on both Android and iOS, **so that** I have a trusted offline record of every vehicle I own.

> Roadmap fidelity note: the ROADMAP `**Goal:**` line for Phase 1 is outcome-form ("The KMP project runs on both platforms with a stable, migration-safe local database, and users can manage their vehicle list"), not user-story form. The story above is the MVP-mode rendering of that goal. Run `/gsd-mvp-phase 1` if the ROADMAP line should itself be rewritten into story form.

<objective>
Turn the deliberately thin list from plan 01-04 into the real D-14 identity-card list: a `VehicleCard` row with a uniform leading visual, a blank-segment-aware meta line and a license-plate chip; an `EmptyState` that guides first use; and a list screen that renders all four UI-SPEC states with the D-19 FAB wired to the Detail and Add destinations.

Purpose: VEH-05 ("user can view a list of all their vehicles on the home screen") is the app's front door and the first thing every other screen navigates back to. This plan is the vertical slice that makes the home screen genuinely readable, so plan 01-07's form and plan 01-09's detail screen both land on a list worth returning to. It also establishes the two reusable primitives (`VehicleTypeCircle`, `LicensePlateChip`) that Detail and the type picker reuse.

Output: a home screen where every vehicle is identifiable at a glance, an empty state that prompts the first add, and a FAB that reaches the Add form.
</objective>
