## Phase Goal

**As a** vehicle owner, **I want to** add, view, edit, and delete my vehicles (with an optional photo) on both Android and iOS, **so that** I have a trusted offline record of every vehicle I own.

> Roadmap fidelity note: the ROADMAP `**Goal:**` line for Phase 1 is outcome-form, not user-story form. The story above is the MVP-mode rendering of it. Run `/gsd-mvp-phase 1` if the ROADMAP line should itself be rewritten into story form.

<objective>
Close VEH-04: a confirmed deletion on the Detail screen removes the vehicle's records **and** its photo file, returns the user to the list with a confirmation message, and never claims a deletion that did not happen.

Purpose: this is the last capability of Phase 1 and the one with the highest consequence per mistake — an unconfirmed delete destroys data, an under-implemented delete leaves the user's personal photos on disk forever, and an optimistically-rendered delete tells the user their data is gone when it is not. The plan therefore spends its budget on three things and nothing else: the confirmation gate, the single-flight failure-preserving state machine, and a real-filesystem test proving the photo file is gone. It also discharges the open obligation carried since plan 01-03 (`delete()` must not orphan photos) by asserting it here rather than trusting the wiring in plan 01-08.

Output: a working, confirmed, irreversible deletion — with a proof that the row and the image both disappear.
</objective>
