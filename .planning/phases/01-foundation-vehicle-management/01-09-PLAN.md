## Phase Goal

**As a** vehicle owner, **I want to** add, view, edit, and delete my vehicles (with an optional photo) on both Android and iOS, **so that** I have a trusted offline record of every vehicle I own.

> Roadmap fidelity note: the ROADMAP `**Goal:**` line for Phase 1 is outcome-form, not user-story form. The story above is the MVP-mode rendering of it. Run `/gsd-mvp-phase 1` if the ROADMAP line should itself be rewritten into story form.

<objective>
Deliver the Detail screen: hero photo or type-circle placeholder, the vehicle name at Display size, a stable five-row field block with the em-dash convention and the plate chip, the reserved "Service history (coming soon)" slot that Phase 2 fills, and an Edit action that reuses the form from plan 01-07.

Purpose: this is the read side of VEH-03 and the anchor for two things later phases depend on. First, it is the designed home of service history — the coming-soon slot is placed now so Phase 2 slots content into an existing structure instead of restyling the screen. Second, it is where Delete will live (plan 01-10) and where Edit is reached from, so the screen's shape must be settled before either lands. The Detail screen also has to be honest about a vehicle that disappears between the list and the detail fetch, which the Flow-driven state handles structurally rather than with a race-prone guard.

Output: a per-vehicle screen that shows every field consistently, degrades to a placeholder for absent values and absent photos, and hands off to the existing form for editing.
</objective>
