## Phase Goal

**As a** vehicle owner, **I want to** add, view, edit, and delete my vehicles (with an optional photo) on both Android and iOS, **so that** I have a trusted offline record of every vehicle I own.

<objective>
Make the storage layer real: the migration-safe WAL SQLDelight schema, the platform driver factories, the domain models, the mapper boundary, the serialized-I/O repository, and the Koin graph that binds them.

Purpose: this is the durable contract every later plan builds on. Three research-verified decisions must land here or they cannot be retrofitted cheaply — the WAL pragma declared in the `.sq` preamble (so the generated `Schema.create()` emits it identically on both drivers), the checked-in schema baseline (so the migration guard has something to diff against), and the single `Dispatchers.IO.limitedParallelism(1)` lane (which prevents concurrent `NativeSqliteDriver` access from aborting the iOS process).

Output: a working data layer — a real write and a real read of a vehicle row through `VehicleRepository`, proven by a test on a real driver — with the schema baseline committed.
</objective>
