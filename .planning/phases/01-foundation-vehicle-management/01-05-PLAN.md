## Phase Goal

**As a** vehicle owner, **I want to** add, view, edit, and delete my vehicles (with an optional photo) on both Android and iOS, **so that** I have a trusted offline record of every vehicle I own.

> **Roadmap fidelity note:** the ROADMAP `**Goal:**` line for this phase is outcome-form, not user-story form. The story above is a faithful restatement of ROADMAP success criteria 1–5 and REQUIREMENTS DAT-01…04 / VEH-01…05 — no scope was invented. If the user wants the canonical story committed to ROADMAP.md, run `/gsd-mvp-phase 1`.

<objective>
Turn this phase's two data-safety promises from assumptions into enforced, proven facts: SQLite runs in WAL mode from first launch on both platforms, and no future schema change can ship without a migration file.

Purpose: RESEARCH verified that both of these fail *silently* by default. The SQLDelight aggregate `verifySqlDelightMigration` task is registered unconditionally and passes green on a repo with no migrations at all, so a naive CI setup satisfies ROADMAP SC-5 on paper while enforcing nothing (Pitfall 1). Likewise WAL can appear to work on one platform and be absent on the other if the pragma lives in platform config instead of SQL (Pitfall 5). This plan closes both holes with a per-source-set task plus a schema-diff guard, and with a real-driver assertion on each platform.

Output: a CI workflow that blocks schema drift, plus a WAL proof test per platform, plus recorded evidence that the guard actually fires.
</objective>
